# Spec

Act as a Senior Product Designer with over 10 years of experience in Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), UX Research, Interaction Design, and Visual Design. You have deep expertise in Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines, Google’s Material Design, and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.

Your task is to help me design **a simple, elegant, and highly usable business directory website called *Index***. The platform serves as the Dominican Republic’s national yellow pages.

## **Design Requirements**

* The website must be **static**, **lightweight**, and **fully usable without JavaScript**, ensuring maximum reliability and simplicity.
* It must load extremely fast, use **clean semantic HTML5**, and rely on **modern, responsive CSS** tailored for mobile-first performance.
* **Clear information hierarchy:** Every screen must guide the user toward the most important actions with logical visual structure.
* **High readability:** Use balanced spacing, simple typography, and high contrast for effortless scanning.
* **Mobile-first layout:** Optimize for small screens first, ensuring all features work naturally on mobile.
* **WCAG accessibility:** All pages must remain usable via keyboard, screen readers, and high-contrast modes.
* **Search prominence:** The search bar should be visually central, instantly recognizable, and always accessible.
* **Clean typography:** Use a modern, legible typeface with consistent sizes and spacing.
* **Compact layout:** Avoid excessive whitespace or oversized hero sections; keep everything tight and efficient.
* **Keep the homepage tight (small) and purposeful**, avoiding oversized headers or empty sections so users immediately see what they can do next.
* **Easy navigation:** Categories, links, and actions must be immediately understandable and reachable with minimal clicks.
* **Clear call-to-action:** Buttons like “Buscar” or “Añadir negocio” should stand out without overwhelming the page.
* **Task clarity:** Each screen must make the user’s next step obvious—search, browse, or contact.
* **Visual-intent alignment:** No element should dominate without purpose; the welcome area must remain subtle relative to core directory functions.

## **Functional Scope**

Create a design and layout for:

* **Navigation:** Provides users with clear, consistent access to the main sections of the directory.
* **Homepage / Welcome:** Introduces the service, highlights key features, and directs users toward search or categories.
* **Search:** Enables quick business lookup by name, service, or location through a prominent and intuitive search box.
* **Category Browsing:** Allows users to explore businesses by well-organized categories such as restaurants, plumbers, or dentists.
* **Top Rated Businesses:** Showcases highly rated or recommended businesses to guide users toward trusted options.
* **Contact Section:** Offers a simple channel for user inquiries, support requests, or business submissions.
* **Footer:** Provides essential links, legal information, and branding in a consistent bottom section.

## **Objective**

Recreate the **structure, layout principles, and user flow** of Fonecta.fi in **English** and localized for the **Dominican Republic**, but:

* More minimal
* Fully static
* No JavaScript
* Simpler and cleaner

Produce a **complete prototype** (HTML + CSS or detailed design specifications) that can be used directly as the basis for coding a fully functional static website.
